XX5 live games guide

Live games use hosted or streamed tables shown in the current XX5 app. Table names, schedules, limits and regional availability may change.

Before joining a table

Confirm the table name, displayed limits, rule panel, connection quality and account balance. Wait for the interface to show the current round state before taking an action.

Connection and stream interruptions

If the stream freezes or the network drops, avoid repeated inputs. Reopen the table, check the recorded result and contact support with the table and time if the account record appears inconsistent.

Conduct and safety

Follow table rules and never share account credentials with a host, mentor or another player. XX5COM will never require your OTP or UPI PIN to explain a table result.

Responsible participation

For adults 18+ only. Set time and spending limits and stop when play is no longer entertainment. See responsible gaming and support.
