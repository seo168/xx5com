Responsible Gaming

Adults 18+ only. Do not participate where prohibited by applicable law.

Keep play controlled

  • Decide a spending and time limit before starting.
  • Never borrow money or use essential household funds.
  • Do not chase losses or treat gaming as income.
  • Take regular breaks and avoid playing when distressed or impaired.

Warning signs

Warning signs include hiding activity, exceeding limits, borrowing money, neglecting responsibilities, or feeling unable to stop.

Take a break

If play is causing harm, stop using the service, remove the app, ask your payment provider about spending controls, and seek help from a qualified local support organisation.

Account assistance

Use our Support page to ask about available account restrictions. Never include your password, OTP code, UPI PIN, or card PIN.
