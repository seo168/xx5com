XX5 Deposit & Withdrawal Methods

XX5 may provide India-friendly channels such as UPI, UPI QR, IMPS, Paytm, and PhonePe. Availability, limits, fees, and processing time depend on the active provider and account status.

Confirm every transaction inside the app. Never transfer money using details received from an unknown message or unofficial account.

Payment options

UPI

Pay from a supported UPI app connected to an eligible Indian bank account.

UPI QR

Scan the QR displayed in the active payment flow and confirm the recipient.

IMPS

Bank transfers may be available for eligible deposits or withdrawals.

Paytm and PhonePe

Supported UPI flows may appear when the corresponding channel is active.

Deposit steps

  1. Sign in through the app obtained from the XX5 download page.
  2. Open the wallet and select an available method.
  3. Review the amount, recipient, limits, and fees.
  4. Complete payment and keep the transaction reference.

Withdrawal information

Options may depend on account verification, provider availability, and platform rules. Enter details only in the official app and keep the request reference until completion.

Payment safety

  • Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, password, or remote-access code.
  • Verify the recipient shown by your bank or UPI app.
  • Do not believe guaranteed-win or guaranteed-return claims.
  • Contact support if a transaction remains unresolved.
  • Set spending limits and read the responsible gaming guide.
